
<p>
    <h2>Pools/Leagues/Sweepstakes</h2>
</p>
<p>
When you register and join any prediction comp, you will automatically be
entered into our 'Global' pool which is for everybody who has
registered and completed their online predictions.
<br/>
<br/>
You can also join or create your own Pool for your colleagues/mates.
<br><br>If you create the pool you have the responsibility of administering the pool and its entrants.
When creating the pool you have the option of specifying whether there is an entry fee and how much the entry fee is.
From here you can also specify how to distribute the 'pot' amongst the winners. i.e. 60% to 1st place, 30% to second and so on.
The league administrator will usually be the person who collects the entry fees and pays the winners of your league/sweepstake.

<br>
<br>
If you create your own pool you can also specify your own scoring system. You will have the option
to pick your own points for correct scores/correct results and correct winning margins. On top of this.

<p>
    <h2>General Information</h2>
